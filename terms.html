What this site is right now

This is a pre-launch demo. The "Reserve your lane" widget and the €5 deposit button run in test mode — no real prescriptions are processed and no real payment is taken. The waitlist form is real: it records your contact details so we can notify you when a lane opens near you.

When a lane goes live

Every operating AIptieka lane will be a licensed pharmacy in its country, staffed by a registered pharmacist, and subject to that country's pharmacy and healthcare regulations. Prescriptions will be verified live against the relevant national e-health register before any medication is dispensed.

Pricing

You will pay the standard pharmacy price for your medication — the same co-pay and insurance rules that apply at any pharmacy apply here. There is no AIptieka membership fee.
