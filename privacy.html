Privacy Policy

Last updated: pilot / pre-launch version. This is a plain-language summary for AIptieka's pre-launch demo site — a full legal privacy policy will be published before any real lane opens.

What we collect

On the waitlist form: your name, email address, nearest city, and whether you checked the box saying you currently hold a prescription. On the interactive "Reserve your lane" demo: nothing is stored — that form is a preview and does not submit anywhere.

Why "I hold a prescription" is treated as sensitive

Ticking that box tells us something about your health, so we treat it as special-category health data, not ordinary marketing data. We only use it to tell you when a lane near you opens. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

Your prescription itself

We never see or store your actual prescription. When a lane is live, a licensed pharmacist checks it directly against your country's national e-health register at the pickup window — nothing is copied into an AIptieka database.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your waitlist information at any time by emailing hello@aiptieka.eu. We will remove it without asking why.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@aiptieka.eu.
